## Scope & Handled Routes

This Next.js App Router application serves **Delivery Personnel & Logistics Staff** and exclusively manages the following route groups:

- **`/delivery`**: Touch-friendly delivery workspace grouped by population, delivery confirmation, collections (`EFECTIVO`, `TRANSFERENCIA`, `CREDITO`), and returns.
- **`/admin`**: Delivery operations, cash reconciliation, and user management dashboard.
